Privacy

Movie Monkey doesn't collect anything about you: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. This app talks only to Movie Monkey on your own computer, over your home network. To find it, the app checks the addresses on your network for Movie Monkey. Your library, what you watch and where you stopped stay on your computer.

The sample films stream from the Internet Archive (archive.org), and their artwork comes from TMDB. Where you stopped in them is kept on this TV only.
