Movie Monkey includes FFmpeg (https://ffmpeg.org), version 9.1, as the
separate programs "ffmpeg" and "ffprobe" inside the app. They get videos ready
for Watch on TV.

How it was built: scripts/build-ffmpeg.sh in https://github.com/shrihari/moviemonkey
